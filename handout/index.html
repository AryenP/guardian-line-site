Guardian Line

A call screener for your parent’s phone

What it is

  • Your parent’s phone rings as it does today. Calls they miss or decline go to an assistant that says it is one.
  • It asks who is calling and why, ends scam calls and sends you a note about the calls it handles.
  • No audio is kept. Nothing is installed on your parent’s phone.

What we’ve measured

In our tests, 1,346 of 1,378 real scam robocall transcripts released by the FTC were flagged, and 3 of 432 genuine test callers were wrongly ended. We have no measured false-alarm rate on real people yet.

What we haven’t

  • How often it catches AI voices on live calls. Our own AI callers ring it each week and we publish the misses.
  • Setup on each carrier is still being tested.
  • No family uses it yet.
