Guardian Line

A call screener for a relative’s phone

What it is

  • Callers not in their contacts reach an assistant that says it is one. Their phone rings only when it connects a caller. Screening before the ring needs an iPhone. On most Android phones and flip phones, the assistant answers the calls they miss or decline. Landlines are not supported yet.
  • It asks who is calling and why, ends scam calls and sends you a note about the calls it handles.
  • No audio is kept. Nothing is installed on their phone.

What we’ve measured

In our tests, 1,346 of 1,378 real scam robocall transcripts released by the FTC were flagged, and 3 of 432 genuine test callers were wrongly ended. We have no measured false-alarm rate on real people yet.

What we haven’t

  • How often it catches AI voices on live calls. We run our own AI callers against it and publish dated results, misses included.
  • Setup with each phone company is still being tested.
  • No family uses it yet.
