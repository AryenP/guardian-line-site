call path

A caller not in their contacts reaches the assistant. It asks who is calling and why, checks for a scam and for a machine, then ends the call, asks the family, puts the caller through to the phone, or takes a message. Contacts ring as usual.

it checksChecks for a scam and a machine

it checks
Whether the caller pushes for money or private details.
Signs of a machine: answers in the pause after the greeting, stops the instant it is interrupted, fails a check it could hear.
Signs of a person: passes a check it could hear, says “mm-hm” or “right” while listening, waits through the pause.
it says
“Quick question: how's the weather where you are?”
it keeps
An AI score and a scam score, each with a confidence level and reasons. Both are estimates, never certain. Kept until you delete your account.

the phone setting

The phone setting

Unknown callers don’t ring

An iPhone silences numbers that aren’t in its contacts and sends them to the assistant. The assistant’s number is saved as a contact, so its connecting call rings.

Screening before the ring needs an iPhone. On most Android phones and flip phones, the assistant answers the calls they miss or decline. Landlines are not supported yet.
