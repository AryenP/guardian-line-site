What the assistant says

These are the exact lines the assistant uses. It speaks the name you entered in step one.

  1. Every call it answers opens with

    “Hi, this is Mom's call assistant. I can pass you through or take a message. Who's calling?”

  2. If a caller asks whether it is a person

    “I'm an automated assistant, not a person. I can pass you through or take a message.”

  3. When a real person calls

    “One moment, I'll try Mom for you.”

  4. If she does not pick up

    “Thanks, I've passed that along to Mom. Goodbye.”

  5. When a caller asks for what scammers ask for

    “I'm not able to help with that. I'll let the family know you called. Goodbye.”

What it never does

  • Ask for or repeat card numbers, Social Security numbers, bank details, one-time codes or passwords.
  • Claim to be a person.
  • Record audio. It keeps a written transcript and deletes it after 30 days.
  • Answer calls from numbers on your family list.

What you get

A note about every call the assistant answers, by notification and email: who called, what they wanted and what happened. Scam calls are marked so you can talk to her about them.
