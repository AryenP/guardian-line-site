The phone setting

Unknown callers don’t ring

Their phone silences numbers that aren’t in its contacts and forwards them to the assistant. The assistant’s number is saved as a contact, so its connecting call rings.

Landlines and most flip phones have no such setting. There the assistant answers missed and declined calls only. A family can choose that on any phone.

An urgent call from an unknown number reaches the assistant first, and the assistant connects it.

Setting it up

  1. 1

    Add a person

    Their first name and their phone number.

  2. 2

    Check it is their phone

    A code goes to their phone by text or call. Type it into the app.

  3. 3

    Set their phone

    Save the assistant’s number as a contact, silence unknown callers, and forward the calls they don’t take. The app walks you through it and places a test call. Still being tested on each carrier.
