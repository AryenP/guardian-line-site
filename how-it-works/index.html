How it works

Their phone rings as usual. When they miss or decline a call, the assistant answers it and writes to you about it.

A call’s path

  1. A call arrives
    What happens
    It rings your parent’s phone, as it does today.
  2. Answered: a normal call
    What happens
    Your parent talks to the caller. The assistant never hears the call.
    It keeps
    Nothing.
    Missed or declined: the assistant
    What happens
    The call goes to the assistant’s number, which answers.
    The caller never hears your parent’s voice.
    It says
    “Hi, this is Ruth's call assistant. I can pass you through or take a message. And may I ask who's calling?”
  3. Asks who and why
    It checks
    The name and the reason the caller gives.
    It says
    “Thanks. Do you need to speak with Ruth now, or shall I pass the message on?”
    “I'm an automated assistant, not a person. I can pass you through or take a message.”
    It keeps
    A written transcript, 30 days by default. You can choose 7 or 90. No audio.
    The name and the reason, with the call record, until you delete your account.
  4. Looks for signs of a scam and of a machine
    It checks
    Whether the caller pushes for money or private details.
    Signs of a machine: replies arrive at the same delay every time, every answer is a long, complete sentence, ignored or over-answered a simple off-topic question.
    Signs of a person: gives short, casual answers, hesitates and corrects itself like a person.
    It says
    “Quick question: how's the weather where you are?”
    It keeps
    An AI score and a scam score, each with a confidence level and plain reasons, until you delete your account. They are estimates, never certain.
  5. Put through, with an announcement
    What happens
    Offered only when nothing sounds wrong. Your parent can hear who is calling and why before the call connects.
    It says
    “One moment, I'll try Ruth for you.”
    Message taken
    What happens
    When it can’t tell, it takes a message for your parent and the app says “Not enough to tell”.
    It says
    “Thanks, I've passed that along to Ruth. Goodbye.”
    It keeps
    A written transcript, 30 days by default. You can choose 7 or 90. No audio.
    Call ended
    What happens
    A call that pushes for money or private details is ended. It never asks for or repeats a card number, a bank detail or a one-time code.
    It says
    “I'm not able to help with that. I'll let the family know you called. Goodbye.”
    It keeps
    A written transcript, 30 days by default. You can choose 7 or 90. No audio.
  6. A note to the family
    What happens
    Who called, what they wanted and what the assistant did, by notification and email. Scam calls are marked.
    A caller who hangs up gets no note.
    It keeps
    Until you delete your account.

When a new number says it’s family

  1. A new number says it’s family

    The assistant hears a caller say they are someone you know.

  2. The caller is put on hold

    “Thanks. I'm checking with Ruth's family. Please hold for a moment.”

  3. Your phone asks

    Is this you calling Ruth?

  4. You answer

    Yes, it's me or No, it's not. The answer is kept with the call until you delete your account.

Checking it is their phone

  1. You enter their number

    Their mobile number, in the app.

  2. A code goes to their phone

    A text message with a code. It is the only text we send.

  3. You type the code

    Typing it into the app confirms the number is theirs.

  4. They agree themselves

    They call their assistant number from their own phone, hear in plain words what will happen to their calls and press 1.

The phone setting

Unknown numbers straight to the assistant

Their phone can be set to send numbers it doesn’t know to the assistant without ringing.

It is off unless the family chooses it, one level down in setup. It is never the default.

A phone set this way won’t ring for an unknown number in an emergency.

Setting it up

  1. Add your parent

    Their first name and their mobile number.

  2. Check it is their phone

    A text with a code goes to their phone. Type it into the app.

  3. Switch it on

    A setting on their phone sends the calls they miss or decline to the assistant. The app walks you through it and places a test call. We are still testing this on each carrier.

It isn’t in the App Store yet.

Leave your email and carrier. We’ll write when a place opens.
