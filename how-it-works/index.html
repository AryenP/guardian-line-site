How it works

Calls your parent misses go to the assistant.

Their phone rings as usual. When they miss or decline a call, the assistant answers it and writes to you about it.

Setting it up

  1. 1Add your parent

    Their first name and their mobile number.

  2. 2Switch it on

    A setting on their phone sends the calls they miss or decline to the assistant. The app walks you through it and places a test call. We are still testing this on each carrier.

Sending every unknown number to the assistant

Their phone can also send every number it doesn’t know straight to the assistant. We don’t turn that on by default, because a phone set that way won’t ring for an unknown number in an emergency.

When a new number says it’s family

It puts the caller on hold and asks the real person.

  • The caller hears
    “Thanks. I'm checking with Mom's family. Please hold for a moment.”
  • Her son’s phone shows

    Is this you calling Mom?

    Yes, it's meNo, it's not

What it actually says

  • Every call it answers
    “Hi, this is Mom's call assistant. I can pass you through or take a message. And may I ask who's calling?”
  • Asked if human
    “I'm an automated assistant, not a person. I can pass you through or take a message.”
  • Putting a caller through
    “One moment, I'll try Mom for you.”
  • Scam call
    “I'm not able to help with that. I'll let the family know you called. Goodbye.”

What it won’t do

Her details
It does not ask for cards, bank details or codes.
Identity
Asked, it says it is not a person.
Audio
Not recorded.
Her voice
Callers the assistant answers never hear it.
AI score
An estimate, never certain. Still being measured.
