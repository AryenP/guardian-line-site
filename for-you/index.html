When you miss a call

  • Your phone rings for everyone, as it does today.
  • A call you miss or decline goes to an assistant.
  • The assistant says it is an assistant. It asks who is calling and why.
  • No sound from your calls is kept.
  • You can turn it off with one code on your phone.
  • Someone in your family sets it up with you.

We are still testing it. Nobody uses it yet.
