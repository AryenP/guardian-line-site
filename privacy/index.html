Privacy policy

Guardian Line is an app for an adult child. It does not run on the parent's phone. The parent's carrier forwards the calls they don't answer to a phone number we operate, and an automated assistant answers those calls. This page explains what we collect to do that, and what we don't.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, to sign you in.
  • Your parent's name, phone number and carrier.
  • Written transcripts and summaries of the calls the assistant answered.
  • Your email address, if you add one.
  • Your device's push notification token.
  • Usage analytics (PostHog).
  • Crash reports (Sentry).

What we don't collect

  • Audio. Calls are not recorded, and our servers store no audio.
  • Your contacts or your location.
  • Anything from your parent's phone.

How long we keep it

  • Call transcripts are deleted after 30 days by default. You can change this in the app's Settings, from 7 to 90 days.
  • Call summaries and call details stay until you delete your account.

Who processes it

We use these companies to run Guardian Line:

  • Twilio: telephony and speech
  • Anthropic: text scoring and call summaries
  • Supabase: database
  • RevenueCat: billing
  • PostHog: usage analytics
  • Sentry: crash reports
  • Resend: email
  • Fly.io and Vercel: hosting

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app's Settings. That removes everything within 24 hours.

California

We do not sell your data. The assistant announces itself at the start of every call and stores no audio.
