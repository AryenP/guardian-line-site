Questions

Are her calls recorded?

No. No audio is stored. The assistant keeps a written transcript of the calls it answers and deletes it after 30 days. You can choose 7, 30 or 90 days in Settings. Calls your parent answers never reach the assistant.

Will callers know they are talking to an assistant?

Yes. The first sentence of every call it answers says so: “Hi, this is Mom's call assistant. I can pass you through or take a message. And may I ask who's calling?” If a caller asks whether it is a real person, it says: “I'm an automated assistant, not a person. I can pass you through or take a message.”

Which calls does the assistant answer?

Only the calls your parent misses or declines. Their phone rings for every caller as it does today. Callers the assistant answers never hear their voice. Their phone can also send every number it doesn’t know straight to the assistant. We don’t turn that on by default, because a phone set that way won’t ring for an unknown number in an emergency.

What happens when someone she knows calls from a new number?

The assistant asks who is calling and, if nothing sounds wrong, offers to put them through. If she does not pick up, it takes a message for you. You can then add the number to the list.

How sure is the AI score?

It is an estimate from words and timing, never certain. When there is too little to go on, the app says “Not enough to tell” and the assistant takes a message. We are still measuring it with our own AI callers on real phone lines, and we publish the results, misses included.

What is in the note I get?

Who called, what they wanted and what the assistant did, by notification and email, shortly after the call. Scam calls are marked so you can talk to her about them.

Does she need to install anything?

No. Setup is a setting on her phone, and the app walks you through it and places a test call. We are still testing setup on each carrier.

What does it do with a scam call?

It tells the caller it cannot help, ends the call and sends you a note saying what the caller asked for.

Can it ask for her card number?

No. It is built so it cannot ask for or repeat card numbers, Social Security numbers, bank details or one-time codes.

How do I turn it off or cancel?

Cancel in the App Store at any time. 7 days free, then $39.99 a year. The plan covers one parent’s phone. The app shows how to undo the setting on her phone, after which her calls ring as before.

For the record

What it won’t do

  • Pretend to be a person. Asked, it says so.
  • Ask for or repeat a card number, a bank detail or a one-time code.
  • Let a caller it answers hear Mom’s voice.
  • Keep audio. It keeps a transcript for 30 days unless you pick 7 or 90.
