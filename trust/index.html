What we store and what we never do

Plain facts about the call, the text and the companies that touch them.

What we never do

  • We never hide what the assistant is. It says it is an assistant in the first sentence of every call.
  • We never record audio. No audio is kept, and the call setup has no recording switched on.
  • We never ask for or read back card numbers, Social Security numbers, bank details, one-time codes or passwords.
  • We never claim to be a person. If a caller asks, the assistant says it is not.
  • We never sell your data, or your parent's.

What we store

  • A written transcript of each call the assistant answered. It is text only.
  • A short summary and the caller number for each call. These stay until you delete your account.
  • Transcripts are deleted after 30 days by default. You can choose 7, 30 or 90 days in Settings.
