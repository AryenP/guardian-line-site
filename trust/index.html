What we keep and never do

The facts about calls, transcripts and the companies that handle them.

Her phoneNumbers on your list

The assistantEvery other caller

What we never do

  • Hide what the assistant is. It says it is an assistant in the first sentence of every call it answers.
  • Record audio. No audio is kept, and recording is switched off in the call setup.
  • Ask for or read back card numbers, Social Security numbers, bank details, one-time codes or passwords.
  • Claim to be a person. Asked directly, the assistant says it is not.
  • Sell your data or your mother’s.

What we keep

  • A written transcript of each call the assistant answered. Text only.
  • A short summary and the caller’s number for each call. These stay until you delete your account.
  • Transcripts are deleted after 30 days by default. You can choose 7, 30 or 90 days in Settings.
