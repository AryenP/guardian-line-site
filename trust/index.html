What we keep

What we keep
what we keephow long
Call audioNot stored. It is heard live and turned into text, never saved.
Written transcript (what the caller and the assistant said), and any message left while the service was down30 days by default. The family can choose 7, 30 or 90 days. Deleted nightly after that.
Call record: time, caller’s number, caller-ID check result, outcome, short summary, the name and reason the caller gaveUntil the account is deleted.
Scores and checks: scam score, AI score with its label, confidence and reasons, the checks the assistant ran, signs of a person, timing measurementsUntil the account is deleted.
Family checks (“Is it really them?”): who the caller claimed to be, the family’s answer and who answeredUntil the account is deleted.
Notes sent to the family (notification and email text)Until the account is deleted.
Facts the family adds about the parent, and notesUntil the family removes them or deletes the account.
Family safe wordOnly as a one-way hash, until removed or the account is deleted.
Trusted and blocked numbersUntil removed. A one-day block is deleted the day after it ends.
Family members: name, phone number, email, push token, role, notification settingsUntil the member leaves, is removed or deletes their account.
Invite codesOnly as a one-way hash. Unused codes are deleted 30 days after they expire.
Coded signals to spot one caller reaching several families (no numbers, no words)8 days.
Early-access list: email, carrier, phone type, optional note, where the visitor came fromUntil the person asks to be removed (no automatic deletion yet).
Sign-up form limits (coded IP address and email)1 day.

Rules it follows on every call it answers

  • 01

    The first thing it says is that it’s an assistant.

  • 02

    It won’t ask for or repeat a card number, bank detail or one-time code.

  • 03

    We don’t sell your data.

who handles it

The companies that handle your data

The companies that handle your data
TwilioCarries the calls, rents the assistant’s phone numbers and runs the live speech connection.
Deepgram (through Twilio)Turns the caller’s speech into text, live.
ElevenLabs (through Twilio)Turns the assistant’s written lines into speech.
AnthropicReads the text of the call to score it and write the summary. It gets text, never audio.
SupabaseDatabase, sign-in and server functions (United States).
Fly.ioRuns the service that answers the calls (United States).
ResendSends email notes.
Expo, with Apple and GoogleDelivers push notifications to family members’ phones.
RevenueCat, with AppleKeeps track of a subscription bought in the app: an account ID and what was bought. No call data.
PostHogCounts how the app and the service are used: taps in the app and, for each call, the outcome, the scores and timings, under an account ID and a family ID. No words from a call and no caller’s number.
SentryReceives error reports from the app and the call service. Call text is dropped and long runs of digits are removed first.
