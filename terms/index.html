What the service does

Guardian Line is for families. You add your parent, the people she knows and up to four other family members. You change settings on her phone so that calls from numbers that are not in her contacts, and calls she misses, declines or can't take, go to a phone number we rent for her. Her phone rings for those callers only when the assistant connects one. That needs an iPhone. On a phone that cannot silence unknown callers, such as most Android phones and flip phones, only the calls she misses or declines go to the assistant. Landlines are not supported yet.

Whether that works for her depends on her carrier and her phone; the app says what has been confirmed for her carrier, and the test call at the end of setup shows whether it worked.

The assistant is automated and says so in its first sentence. It asks who is calling and why. It puts a stranger through only after it sees a sign that a person is on the line; otherwise it takes a message. A caller who says they are family can be checked with your family ("Is it really them?") or with your family's safe word. It ends calls that look like scams and sends your family a short note about each call it answers.

Your parent's agreement

Set Guardian Line up only for a parent who knows about it and agrees. A code is sent to her phone, and entering it in the app is recorded as her agreement, so you confirm that she has agreed. [A step where she confirms this herself by calling her assistant number from her own phone and pressing 1 is being built.] You must be allowed to change the settings on her phone.

The plan

One family plan covers up to two parents' phones and up to five family members. It is billed monthly or yearly, after a free trial, and you can buy it in the App Store or on our website. The price is shown before you pay. Nobody is charged during early access.

A plan bought in the App Store is billed by Apple and renews until you cancel it in your App Store settings. A plan bought on our website renews until you cancel it in your account. When you cancel, undo the setting on your parent's phone (the app shows how) so her calls stop going to the assistant.

Using it fairly

  • Don't use Guardian Line to harass, deceive or record anyone, or to break the law.
  • Don't try to make the assistant ask for or say things it isn't built for, and don't interfere with the service.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. Invite family members with an invite code instead.

What it can't promise

Screening is automated and it makes mistakes. It can miss a scam call. It can hold back a real caller and take a message instead. An AI score is an estimate, never a certainty, and is shown with how confident it is.

It only answers calls that reach it, so it can't help with calls from numbers in her contacts or with calls that her phone does not forward to it. It is not an emergency service and does not handle 911 calls.
