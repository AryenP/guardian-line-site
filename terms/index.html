Terms of service

What the service does

Guardian Line is for families. You add your parent, the people she knows and up to four other family members. You change a setting on her phone so that calls she misses, declines or can't take go to a phone number we rent for her. Calls from numbers on your family's list ring her phone as usual.

The setup is designed so that callers who are not on your list reach our assistant before they reach her. Whether that works for her depends on her carrier and her phone; the app says what has been confirmed for her carrier, and the test call at the end of setup shows whether it worked.

The assistant is automated and says so in its first sentence. It asks who is calling and why. It puts a stranger through only after it sees a sign that a person is on the line; otherwise it takes a message. A caller who says they are family can be checked with you ("Is this you?") or with your family's safe word. It ends calls that look like scams and sends your family a short note about each call it answers.

Your parent's agreement

Set Guardian Line up only for a parent who knows about it and agrees. She confirms this herself by calling her assistant number from her own phone and pressing 1. [Being built. Until it is live, you confirm that she has agreed.] You must be allowed to change the settings on her phone.

The plan

One family plan covers up to two parents' phones and up to five family members. It is billed monthly or yearly, after a free trial, and you can buy it in the App Store or on our website. The price is shown before you pay. Nobody is charged during early access.

A plan bought in the App Store is billed by Apple and renews until you cancel it in your App Store settings. A plan bought on our website renews until you cancel it in your account. When you cancel, undo the setting on your parent's phone (the app shows how) so her calls stop going to the assistant.

Using it fairly

  • Don't use Guardian Line to harass, deceive or record anyone, or to break the law.
  • Don't try to make the assistant ask for or say things it isn't built for, and don't interfere with the service.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. Invite family members with an invite code instead.

What it can't promise

Screening is automated and it makes mistakes. It can miss a scam call. It can hold back a real caller and take a message instead. An AI score is an estimate, never a certainty, and is shown with how confident it is.

It only answers calls that reach it, so it can't help with calls from numbers on your list or with calls that forwarding does not send to it. It is not an emergency service and does not handle 911 calls.

Ending

You can stop at any time: cancel the plan, undo the setting on your parent's phone and delete your account in Settings. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms.
