Terms of service

The service

Guardian Line is an app for adult children. You add your parent and the people they know, and make a one-time setting on your parent's phone. From then on, calls from numbers on your list ring their phone as usual, and calls from every other number go to a number we operate. An automated assistant answers those calls, says that it is an assistant, and then puts the caller through to your parent, takes a message, or ends calls that look like scams. We send you a short note about each call.

Your subscription

Guardian Line costs $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Apple bills it through your App Store account, and it renews every year until you cancel. You can cancel any time in your App Store subscription settings.

When you cancel, undo the setting on your parent's phone (the app shows how) so their calls stop going to the assistant.

Using it fairly

  • Set Guardian Line up only on a phone whose owner knows about it and agrees to it.
  • Don't use it to harass or deceive anyone, or to break the law.
  • Don't try to make the assistant ask for or say things it isn't built for, and don't interfere with the service.

What it can't promise

Screening is automated. The assistant can miss a scam call, and it can mistake a real caller for a scammer. It only hears calls from numbers that are not on your list, so it can't help with calls from numbers on the list. It is not an emergency service.
